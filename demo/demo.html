There is no decryption in this page. The key is not recovered from the digits, and the server never reverses them. It recomputes HMAC-SHA1(key, counter) and compares the result. That is also why the digits change when the counter changes, and why the digits alone tell you nothing about the key.

RFC 4226 (HOTP) and RFC 6238 (TOTP). Demo key is the published RFC 4226 test vector, not a credential.
